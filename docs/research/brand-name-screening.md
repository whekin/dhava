# Public brand name screening

Date: 2026-08-02

## Context

`Dhava` is already used by an active GPS fitness-tracking app in the same category. Its public positioning includes walking, running, cycling, hiking, routes, social features, challenges, wearables, and a premium subscription. That makes an exact-name launch unnecessarily vulnerable to store-search confusion, word-of-mouth ambiguity, and a later forced rename, even though Android package names can remain technically distinct.

The replacement should fit the actual product rather than sound like a generic activity tracker:

- downhill- and segment-first;
- offline recording with GPS, IMU, and barometer;
- honest accuracy and timing uncertainty;
- gates, lines, splits, airtime, and multi-ride refinement;
- capable of expanding from MTB to other gravity sports.

## Evaluation criteria

Candidates were judged on distinctiveness, pronunciation in English/Russian, product fit, searchability, and ability to stretch beyond MTB. Collision checks covered exact-name web search and indexed Google Play/App Store results. `.com` registration was checked through Verisign RDAP on 2026-08-02.

This is preliminary product research, not legal trademark clearance. Domain and store availability can change at any time.

## Shortlist

| Candidate | What it communicates | Strengths | Weaknesses | Preliminary collision signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gravtrace** | gravity + trace | Closest to the product architecture: gravity sports and a measured/corrected line. International, technical without saying “fitness,” can cover MTB and snow. | Slightly engineered; the consonant join needs a visual identity and pronunciation test. | No exact app/store result found; `gravtrace.com` returned RDAP 404 at check time. |
| **Daghma** | an invented shortening inspired by Georgian `დაღმართი` (*daghmarti*, downhill/slope) | Strong Georgian origin story, short, ownable, relevant to descent, and not limited to bikes. | `gh` pronunciation is not obvious abroad; the shortened form is a coined brand, not the Georgian word itself; the string also occurs as a surname/name elsewhere. | No exact app/store result found; `daghma.com` returned RDAP 404 at check time. |
| **LoamSignal** | trail surface + sensor/data signal | Vivid MTB personality; connects forest riding with the app’s sensor work and changing GPS quality. Easy to understand and visually rich. | Narrows the brand toward dirt/MTB and is less natural for snow or urban stairs; longer than the others. | No exact app/store result found; `loamsignal.com` returned RDAP 404 at check time. |
| **Gravra** | invented gravity-adjacent word | Short, pronounceable enough, flexible across gravity sports, and visually strong. | Meaning is not self-evident; likely needs a descriptor such as “gravity ride tracking” for early acquisition. | No exact app/store result found; `gravra.com` returned RDAP 404 at check time. |

## Recommendation

**Gravtrace** is the strongest product name in this pass. It describes the unique value better than the category: not merely recording a ride, but recovering a trustworthy trace through gravity terrain. It also leaves room for snowboarding or other descent sports.

**Daghma** is the strongest brand story if Georgian identity is important. Before choosing it, test the spoken form with several English speakers and a native Georgian copywriter; the intended derivation should be described as inspiration, not translation.

**LoamSignal** is the best deliberately MTB-first option. Choose it only if being unmistakably mountain-bike-specific for the next several years is more valuable than a broader gravity-sports umbrella.

**Gravra** is the safest blank canvas, but it gives up the immediate “measured line” meaning of Gravtrace.

Suggested public-name test:

1. Say each name in Russian, English, and Georgian without showing the spelling.
2. Ask five riders to spell it after hearing it once.
3. Show an activity share card and ask what kind of product they expect.
4. Run formal trademark clearance in the intended launch territories before paying for identity work.
5. Secure the chosen domain and store/social handles before announcing the rename.

## Rejected directions

- **Biliki** — an active Tbilisi-based offline trail/tour app already uses the name; too adjacent geographically and functionally.
- **Kvali** — an active genealogy/DNA product uses the name.
- **Dineba** — used by an active Tbilisi cultural/archive initiative.
- **Nakadi** — used by Zalando’s event-streaming platform and other technology products.
- **DropTrace** — used by logistics/tracking products.
- **Downtrace** — used by an active edge-AI/defence company.
- **Dropmark** — long-running web/iOS product by Oak Studios.
- **SplitSignal** — active SEO experimentation platform.
- **Tracefall / Linefall** — active or announced games and software use the strings.
- **Gravlo / Gravta** — current app/company collisions.
- **Gatecraft / Splitmark / LineProof / DirtSignal** — existing software or active businesses.
